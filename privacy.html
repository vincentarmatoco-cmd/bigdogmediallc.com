Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Big Dog Media LLC ("Big Dog Media", "we", "us") handles information collected through this website, bigdogmediallc.com. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us. When you submit our contact form, we receive the information you enter — typically your name, business, budget range, and your message. We do not require an account, and we do not knowingly collect payment details, government IDs, or sensitive personal data through this site.

Our web host may automatically log standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability. We do not use this to identify individual visitors.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss a potential project.
  • To follow up about the services you asked about.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

Third-party services

To run this site we rely on a few trusted providers who may process limited data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms — delivers your contact-form submission to our email inbox.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on the site; loading them shares your IP address with Google.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts and serves the website.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms.

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

How long we keep it

We keep contact-form messages only as long as needed to respond to you and manage our business relationship, then delete them on request or when they are no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you sent us at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Children

This site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
